# H35D: frozen-teacher rank-fusion diagnostic

Registered before H35D real-data scoring, 2026-09-06. This is an adaptive
follow-up to H35C and the RelEns paper discussion, not a fresh holdout claim.

Question: does equal-weight candidate-rank fusion preserve useful information
that the current mean-raw-logit teacher target loses? This is a diagnostic for
future single-model distillation, not an inference-ensemble submission.

- Freeze the released BioKG teachers `sparse_s0.pt` through `sparse_s9.pt` and
  the original student `dist_T2_s0.pt`. Verify release, data and H35C receipt
  hashes and entity/relation indexing. Local GTX 1080 Ti only.
- Score FULL VALID: 162,886 triples, both directions (tail then head), the
  same 500 official negatives and true candidate. Process chunks of 128.
  No TEST reads, training, optimizer, gradients, feature building, weight
  fitting, hyperparameter sweep or checkpoint selection. LR is N/A.
- Use one fp32/complex64 scorer for all 501 candidate columns. No AMP/TF32.
  Frozen mean logits: arithmetic mean of ten scaled raw logits, in seed order.
  Rank fusion: each teacher ranks all candidates descending, average ranks for
  exact ties; average those ranks equally, then rerank. Accumulate doubled
  ranks as integers to preserve exact fusion ties. Negative sum of doubled
  ranks is ranking-equivalent to negative mean rank. Never average the ten
  positive reciprocal ranks and call that a fused model.
- Use OGB optimistic/pessimistic average-rank evaluation for each teacher,
  both fusions, and the original student. Score the student directly and
  compare with the archived H35 full-validation ranks. No relation weights.
- Primary contrast: rank fusion minus mean-logit fusion, MRR. Paired bootstrap
  over whole triples (head/tail kept together), 1,000 replicates, seed 3512,
  95% interval. Investigate rank-target KD only if delta >= +0.001 and lower
  bound > 0. This gate does not authorize training or promise compression.
- Secondary descriptive results: individual-teacher MRR, student gaps,
  Hits@1/10, direction/relation/family slices, recovered/lost student top-1
  queries, pairwise teacher rank agreement and positive top-1 disagreement.
  These slices do not select weights or additional configurations. Confidence
  intervals describe query variability, not training-seed uncertainty or
  protection against adaptive validation reuse.
- Save per-query ranks and indexing, protocol/source/input hashes, timed
  progress, unchanged parameter digests and absent-gradient checks. Do not
  store full candidate-score caches. No model checkpoint is written.
- Synthetic tests must cover ties, candidate permutations, duplicate candidate
  scores, rank-fusion monotone invariance, scorer parity, frozen parameters,
  and selective split loading. Full-size synthetic GPU smoke before scoring.
- Stop after this diagnostic and record the outcome. Do not automatically
  launch mixed-geometry or neighborhood-context B experiments.

Caveats: these teachers share a model family. This is not a reproduction of
RelEns's heterogeneous bases or validation-tuned relation weights. Candidate
ranks over 501 official candidates differ from ranks over 4,097 training
candidates; any future KD target needs its own TRAIN-only design and matched
control. Validation candidates are evaluation inputs, never training data.
